ℹ️ How to Play

Welcome to Explorer!

The map is covered in a dense Fog of War. It's up to you to uncover the hidden world by traveling to new locations!

📍 Moving Around

  • Simulate Mode (👆): Click anywhere on the map to teleport your player dot.
  • GPS Mode: Turn off Simulate Mode in Settings to track your actual real-world location. The map will follow you automatically!
  • Pan Mode (✋): Toggle this mode at the top to drag and scroll the map without moving your player.

🔍 Discoveries

  • Whenever you enter the radius of a hidden point of interest (POI), you will discover it and earn points.
  • You can change your Discovery Threshold (radius) in the Settings panel. A smaller radius is more challenging!

🏆 Leaderboard

  • As you earn points, you'll rank up against rival explorers and unlock new badges (from Sprout 🌱 to Legend 🏆).
